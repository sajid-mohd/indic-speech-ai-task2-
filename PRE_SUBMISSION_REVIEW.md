## Evidence and limitations

- FLEURS test: 100 unique Hindi/Kannada source utterances, each clean and synthetic 10 dB AWGN; 600 predictions across three decoding methods.
- QA inference: 100 Hindi synthetic-TTS examples, executed on CPU; mean pipeline latency 202.47 s and mean pipeline RTF 14.502.
- Reproducibility spot check: 60/60 paired transcripts matched across a 10-utterance subset.
- Offline unit tests pass in the package build environment.
- Response quality counts are provisional rubric-based/AI-assisted estimates (12/100 correct or adequate, 24/100 hallucination flags, 44/100 visible truncation flags). They are not independent human annotations; official human-review coverage remains 0/100. Do not describe them as human-verified.
- Kannada ASR performance is very poor (WER around or above 100%) and should be presented as a limitation, not hidden.
